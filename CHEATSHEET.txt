STEP 1 — one kernel, end to end                                        (~70 s)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  location:  /home/michalis/nvidia-demo/classes

  scripted:
    bash ~/nvidia-demo/0-tiled.sh

  by hand:
    tornado --classpath . TiledMM 1024 10

    nsys profile -t cuda --force-overwrite=true -o /tmp/t \
         tornado --classpath . TiledMM 1024 10
    nsys stats --force-export=true --report cuda_gpu_kern_sum     --format csv /tmp/t.nsys-rep
    nsys stats --force-export=true --report cuda_gpu_mem_time_sum --format csv /tmp/t.nsys-rep

    tornado --printKernel --classpath . TiledMM 1024 3

  without the launcher (same output, plain JVM):
    java @$TORNADOVM_HOME/tornado-argfile -Dtornado.printKernel=True -cp . TiledMM 1024 3

  expect:  first execution ~100000 us (JIT), steady state ~1100 us
           kernel 333 us + transfers 757 us = 1090 us  ~= the wall clock
           140 lines of CUDA C, validation PASSED


STEP 2 — TornadoVM vs hand-written CUDA                                (~90 s)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  location:  /home/michalis/nvidia-demo/classes

  scripted:
    bash ~/nvidia-demo/1-ladder.sh

  by hand:
    nsys profile -t cuda --force-overwrite=true -o /tmp/tv \
         tornado --classpath . MatMulLadder 2048 10
    nsys stats --force-export=true --report cuda_gpu_kern_sum --format csv /tmp/tv.nsys-rep

    nsys profile -t cuda --force-overwrite=true -o /tmp/cu \
         ~/nvidia-demo/matmul_ladder_cuda 2048 10
    nsys stats --force-export=true --report cuda_gpu_kern_sum --format csv /tmp/cu.nsys-rep

  compare the Med (ns) column:
    kcRegisterTiled  vs  registerTiled      kcTiled vs tiled      naive vs naive

  expect:  1. naive            3403 us  vs  3388 us   1.005x
           2. tiled            2597 us  vs  2590 us   1.002x
           3. register-tiled    499 us  vs   486 us   1.027x

  DO NOT quote the ladder's own wall clock against the CUDA binary — TornadoVM's
  includes host dispatch and three 16 MB transfers, the CUDA binary's does not.


STEP 3 — the generated CUDA for the register-tiled kernel              (~40 s)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  location:  /home/michalis/nvidia-demo/classes

  scripted:
    bash ~/nvidia-demo/2-printkernel.sh

  by hand:
    tornado --printKernel --classpath . MatMulLadder 512 3 | less
        then inside less:   /kcRegisterTiled

    # or pull just that kernel out
    tornado --printKernel --classpath . MatMulLadder 512 3 2>/dev/null \
      | awk '/__global__ void kcRegisterTiled/,/^\}/'

  expect:  eight  f_NNN = *(( float *) ul_NNN);   <- global loads
           then   adf_5[i_NN] = f_NNN;            <- shared stores


STEP 4 — the SASS, and the one difference left                         (~40 s)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  location:  /home/michalis/nvidia-demo/classes

  scripted:
    bash ~/nvidia-demo/3-sass.sh

  by hand:
    tornado --classpath . MatMulLadder 512 3
    ls $TORNADOVM_HOME/var/cuda-codecache/device-0-0/
    cuobjdump -sass $TORNADOVM_HOME/var/cuda-codecache/device-0-0/kcRegisterTiled-*.cubin \
      | grep -oE '\b(LD|LDG|STS)\b' | tr -d '\n'; echo

    nvcc -arch=sm_89 -O3 -std=c++17 -cubin -o /tmp/hand.cubin ~/nvidia-demo/MatMulLadder.cu
    cuobjdump -sass /tmp/hand.cubin | grep -oE '\b(LD|LDG|STS)\b' | tr -d '\n'; echo

  expect:  TornadoVM         LD  x8 then STS x8    (generic LD)
           hand-written      LDG x8 then STS x8    (LDG)
           same schedule, different load instruction


GOTCHAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * nsys stats NEEDS --force-export=true, or the 2nd run fails with a usage message
  * the ladder needs >= 2 executions (medianOf divides by executions-1)
  * steps 3 and 4 do not need nsys at all
